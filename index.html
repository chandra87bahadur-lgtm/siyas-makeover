About Siya’s Makeover

Your Beauty & Learning Destination

Siya’s Makeover is a beauty parlour and academy in Ayodhya Nagar, Bhopal, offering services for bridal occasions, parties, hair styling, skin care, and nail care.

Whether you are preparing for a special event or looking to enjoy a little self-care, get in touch to discuss the service that suits your needs. The academy side of the business also makes Siya’s Makeover a destination for beauty-focused learning.
